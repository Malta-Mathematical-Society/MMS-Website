_Beyond the Jacobian Conjecture_ will be a panel discussion about AI in Mathematics, with panelists Prof. Joel Azzopardi, Dr Alexander Farrugia, Prof. Josef Lauri and Prof. Joseph Muscat, and moderated by Louie Cotter.

This is organised in light of recent developements of LLM's producing proofs to old mathematical problems, notably the counterexample generated by a group of AI agents to the Jacobian Conjecture.
