# Join the Malta Mathematical Society

Membership is open to anyone passionate about mathematics — from sixth form and university students to researchers, educators, and enthusiasts.

### Membership Perks & Benefits
* **Events & Socials**: Free or discounted admission to MMS talks, workshops, and socials.
* **Community & Voting Rights**: Full voting rights at the Annual General Meeting (AGM) to help shape the direction of the Society.
* **Networking**: Connect with fellow students, local and international mathematicians, and academics.
* **Support Local Mathematics**: Help fund outreach events, public lectures, and student competitions across Malta.
