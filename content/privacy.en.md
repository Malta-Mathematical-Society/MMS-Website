### Who We Are
The **Malta Mathematical Society** ("we", "us", "our") operates this website and acts as the data controller for your personal data. 

**Contact Email:** maltamathematicalsociety@gmail.com

### What We Collect & Why (Legal Basis)
When you fill out our newsletter form, we collect:
1. **Email address**
2. **Language preference** (English or Maltese)
3. **Topic interests** (Newsletters, Comics, Events)

**Legal Basis:** We process this data based on your explicit **consent** (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you provide when submitting the subscription form.

### Who Processes Your Data & Transfers
We use **MailerLite** to manage our subscriber list and dispatch emails. MailerLite acts as our data processor. 

If data is processed outside the European Economic Area (EEA), such transfers are safeguarded using standard data protection clauses approved by the European Commission. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

### How Long We Keep Your Data
We retain your personal data for as long as you remain subscribed to our communications. If you unsubscribe or request erasure, your data will be deleted from our active mailing lists without undue delay.

### Security Measures
We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

### Your Rights Under the GDPR
Under the General Data Protection Regulation, you have the following rights:
* **Right to Withdraw Consent:** You can withdraw consent at any time by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link at the bottom of any email.
* **Right of Access & Rectification:** You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you or request corrections to inaccurate details.
* **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** You can ask us to permanently delete your data.
* **Right to Restrict or Object:** You can request that we restrict or stop processing your data under certain circumstances.
* **Right to Data Portability:** You can request a portable copy of the data you provided to us.
* **Right to Lodge a Complaint:** If you believe your privacy rights have been infringed, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, such as the **Office of the Information and Data Protection Commissioner (IDPC)** in Malta.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at `maltamathematicalsociety@gmail.com`.

### Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated date.
