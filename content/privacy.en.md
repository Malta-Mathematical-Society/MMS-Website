### Who We Are
**Malta Mathematical Society** (`maltamathematicalsociety@gmail.com`) operates this site and controls your subscriber data.

### What We Collect & Why
At the submission of forms, we collect the following to send you what you want in your language:
1. your email address
2. your language choice (English or Maltese) 
3. your topic interests (Newsletters, Comics, Events)


### Who Processes Your Data
We use **MailerLite** to securely store our list and dispatch emails.
We do not share your details with third parties for marketing.

### Your Rights & Control
* **Unsubscribe:** Every email contains a one-click unsubscribe link at the bottom.
* **Access & Erasure:** You can email us anytime to see what details we store about you, correct inaccuracies, or ask us to completely wipe your data from our systems.
